IgnoreWhy

Paste a .gitignore and a list of paths. IgnoreWhy says which are ignored, and which line decided it: last match wins, negation, directory-only rules, and the parent-directory trap.
One .gitignore at the repo root (no nested .gitignore files, no .git/info/exclude or global excludes), case-sensitive matching. Follows the gitignore manual (read in full) and was compared with real git check-ignore on thousands of random paths. A path without a trailing slash is treated as a file. Nothing is sent anywhere.
